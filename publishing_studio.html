Section 2.2 • Foundational Theory

Stakeholder Capitalism & Integrated Governance

Modern institutional governance requires orchestrating complex equilibriums between immediate shareholder capital returns and long-range stakeholder externalities. As corporations navigate multi-jurisdictional compliance regimens and ESG reporting standards, the traditional Friedmanite doctrine yields to integrated models where employees, supplier federations, and environmental ecosystems hold recognized equity stakes in corporate longevity.

lightbulb Key Concept: Freeman's Stakeholder Model (1984)

R. Edward Freeman posited that managerial strategy must prioritize any group or individual who can affect or is affected by the achievement of an organization's objectives. In digital and intellectual property-intensive firms, this extends to consumer data stewardship and algorithmic accountability panels.
