Stakeholder Capitalism & Integrated Governance

Estimated Read Time: 4 mins • Bloom Level: Analyzing (L4)

Modern enterprise doctrine increasingly acknowledges that fiduciary duty extends beyond quarterly shareholder maximization. Integrated governance posits that long-term value creation depends directly upon reciprocal multi-constituent alignment.

Theoretical Foundation

Freeman’s Stakeholder Concept (1984)

Edward Freeman asserted that managerial stewardship must synchronize suppliers, communities, employees, and financiers. Value leakage occurs when one cohort is systematically subordinated.
